简单来说：本游戏不收集、不上传你的任何数据；所有对局、棋谱、设置与导入的音乐只保存在你自己的设备上。

中文版

1. 我们收集哪些信息？

我们不收集任何个人信息。本游戏没有账号系统、没有登录、没有广告、没有内购，也不包含任何第三方统计或分析 SDK。游戏不会向任何服务器发送你的对局、设备或使用数据。

2. 游戏数据保存在哪里？

以下数据仅保存在你的设备本地（浏览器 localStorage / IndexedDB，或 Android 应用私有存储）：对局自动存档与本局棋谱；你创建的棋谱库；游戏设置（人机难度、音量、自动存档开关等）；你导入的背景音乐文件；本地统计数据。这些数据仅用于在你的设备上提供游戏功能与「继续上局」体验，不会上传，也不会提供给任何第三方。

3. 背景音乐导入

你可以从设备中选择本地音频文件作为背景音乐。该文件仅被读取到本应用本地存储中用于播放，不会上传、不会再分发。音乐文件的版权由你自行负责。

4. 网络与权限

游戏完全离线运行，不需要联网。Android 版仅声明 INTERNET 权限以兼容部分系统 WebView 运行环境，但游戏代码不会发起任何网络请求，也不会上传数据。

5. 儿童隐私

本游戏不含广告、社交、聊天、UGC 或付费内容，适合全年龄游玩。由于不收集任何个人信息，不涉及儿童个人信息收集问题。

6. 数据删除

你可以随时在「设置 → 存档与备份」中删除自动存档与棋谱；卸载应用或在系统设置中清除应用数据，会一并删除全部本地数据。

7. 政策变更与联系方式

如果未来版本增加联网功能，我们会更新本政策并在游戏内与商店页同步说明。如有隐私相关问题，请联系：boardhub.games1@gmail.com（也是 TapTap 商店页公示的客服邮箱），我们会在 7 个工作日内回复。开发者名称：BoardHubGames。

Privacy Policy

Version v1.2.0 · Effective 2026-10-20 · Applies to BoardHub for TapTap International (Android)

1. What information do we collect?

We do not collect any personal information. The game has no accounts, no login, no ads, no in-app purchases, and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. The game does not send your matches, device data or usage data to any server.

2. Where is game data stored?

Auto-saves and game records, your saved records, game settings, imported background music and local statistics are stored only on your device (browser localStorage / IndexedDB, or the Android app's private storage). This data is used only to provide game functionality and the "continue last game" experience, and is never uploaded or shared with any third party.

3. Imported background music

Music files you import are only read/copied into the app's local storage for playback; they are never uploaded or redistributed. You are responsible for the rights to any music you import.

4. Network and permissions

The game runs fully offline. The Android build declares the INTERNET permission solely for compatibility with some system WebView environments; the game makes no network requests and uploads no data.

5. Children's privacy

The game contains no ads, social features, chat, user-generated content or paid content, and is suitable for all ages. Because no personal information is collected, there is no collection of children's personal data.

6. Data deletion & contact

You can delete auto-saves and records in Settings → Saves & Backup, or delete all local data by uninstalling the app or clearing app data. For privacy questions, contact us at boardhub.games1@gmail.com (also the support email shown on our TapTap store page); we reply within 7 business days. Developer: BoardHubGames.
